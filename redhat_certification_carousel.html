01Level I only exists in OpenShift and does not count toward RHCSA, RHCE, or RHCA in any track.
02Passing any exam renews all certifications at the same level and below, across all tracks simultaneously.
03No certifications were taken away. Former RHCA in Infrastructure → automatically RHCA in Enterprise Linux.
04Certifications that were current but no longer meet new requirements remain valid under the new scheme.
05EX200 (RHCSA) is the only exam shared across two tracks: Level II in Enterprise Linux and Level II in Ansible.
06EX294 (Ansible) now has its own dedicated track certification — a new addition in this scheme.
07Some retired exams still count toward the RHCA under the new rules.
